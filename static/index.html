colourise-net / demo

Bring greyscale photos to colour

A small PyTorch U-Net, trained on CIFAR-10 and served with FastAPI.

  1. 01Convert to greyscale
  2. 02Shrink to 32×32
  3. 03U-Net predicts the colours
  4. 04Return 32×32, or resize to full size

Full-res trick: keep the model's colour, but swap its blurry brightness for the original photo's sharp brightness.
